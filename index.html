01 · About

From airbags to out-of-order CPUs

I like hardware that has to be right the first time. At Bosch, that meant airbag controllers, where a missed fault isn't a bug report, it's a safety issue. I spent two and a half years on HIL and SIL benches injecting faults, reading DTCs over UDS and chasing signals with scopes, logic analyzers and JTAG until each failure had a root cause.

Along the way I kept going deeper into the chip: an image-dehazing accelerator on an FPGA in Taiwan, a UVM testbench for an AMBA APB slave, and now a cycle-accurate out-of-order processor simulator at NC State.

My goal is to bring that validation-first mindset to pre-silicon design verification and post-silicon validation.
